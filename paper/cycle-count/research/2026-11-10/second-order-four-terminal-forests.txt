Four-terminal affine-join forests: finite witnesses and open gap question.
10 November 2026.

For a connected graph G and an even terminal set T, its affine T-join space
has 2^beta(G) elements. Let F_T(G) count its acyclic elements.
The proven two-terminal theorem gives either F_T=2^beta or
F_T<=(15/16)*2^beta when |T|=2.

For four terminals, simple graphs attain
  F_T=2^h-1 at h=3,4,5,6,7.
These are exact finite witnesses, not certified global maxima.
The witnesses are Hamiltonian four-poles: terminal degrees are two, and
all other degrees are three. The JSON files contain complete edge lists.
An independent affine cycle-basis enumeration checks every join.
It gives the unique cyclic join explicitly for each witness.

Any four-terminal uniform gap must be at most 1/128.
This restriction persists at arbitrarily large rank. Start with the rank-seven
witness, choose a terminal t, and take a disjoint diamond K4 minus ab.
Join t to its degree-two terminal a by a bridge. Replace t in T by b.
Every new T-join uses the bridge, since the diamond shore has one terminal.
Its restriction to the diamond is an a-b join. All four such joins are paths.
Thus the rank increases by two, and both forest and total join counts multiply
by four. Repetition preserves forest density 127/128 in simple cubic four-poles.

This does not refute a positive gap depending only on |T|.
The natural rank-increasing extension search stopped at rank-seven.
Each step subdivides two edges and joins the two new vertices.
Keep the extension only when it has exactly one cyclic T-join.
The retained labelled counts were 8,44,132,192 at ranks 4,5,6,7.
No rank-eight extension survived. The search width limit was never reached.
This only describes extensions of the chosen rank-three witness.
It does not exclude unrelated witnesses or other extension operations.

Reproduce the independent audit:
  python3 check_second_order_four_terminal_forests.py
The checked output is second-order-four-terminal-forest-checks.json.

Open question: for each fixed even t, is there a delta_t>0 such that
F_T(G)=2^beta or F_T(G)<=(1-delta_t)*2^beta whenever |T|=t?
The present work proves delta_2=1/16 sharply and forces delta_4<=1/128.
It proves no lower bound for delta_4.

Six-terminal side experiment.
The same extension search starts from the rank-three witness after replacing
one path edge by a three-edge path and marking its two new vertices.
This changes four terminals to six without changing rank or the unique bad join.
A width1000 search found exactly-one-cyclic-join witnesses through rank10.
No rank11 extension of the retained rank10 beam survived.
The beam bound was reached, so this is not exhaustive beyond its initial ranks.
Independent Python enumeration checked every join in each printed witness.
In particular, any six-terminal uniform gap is at most1/1024.
The bridge-plus-diamond extension preserves that density at larger ranks.
