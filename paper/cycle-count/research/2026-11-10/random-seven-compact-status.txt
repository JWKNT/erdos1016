Exact:
second-order-random-seven-prism-blockers.tex proves the prism-blocker criterion.
A missing-pair graph on six ports blocks every matching of some triangular
prism exactly when it crosses every balanced partition into two triples.
Equivalently, it contains a three-edge star, a four-vertex path, or three
disjoint edges.
The proof uses Hall's theorem and three explicit prism placements.
path_stability independently audited the full proof.

It finds exactly ten maximal nonblocking graphs, each two disjoint triangles.
