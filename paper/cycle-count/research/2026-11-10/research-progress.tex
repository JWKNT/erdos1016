\begin{abstract}
We develop several consequences of the November 10 manuscript.
The general fixed-gap path bound improves from order $b/\log^2 b$ to order $b/\log b$.
For two-terminal series-parallel networks, a sharp additive estimate gives length at least $b-O_\varepsilon(1)$.
A small-cut inverse theorem describes pair correlations and conditional concentration near the one-half bound.
For random graphs at the Alon--Krivelevich density, the pancyclicity excess above $\log_2 n$ has order $\ln n/\ln\ln n$.
The lower coefficient is $2/3-o(1)$ and the upper coefficient is $2+o(1)$.
The upper proof refines the published construction and corrects several intermediate statements.
For four-regular multigraphs, we prove a doubled-edge reduction and verify the exact conjectured bound through nine vertices.
The general linear path and exact four-regular questions remain unresolved here.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Scope and verification status}
