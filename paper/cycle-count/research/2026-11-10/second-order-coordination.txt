POTENTIAL STRONGER SMALL-CUT LEMMA — UNPROVED
For k disjoint cyclic regions with cut size at most fixed d, can the forest
probability be bounded by 1/2+C_d exp(-c_d k)?
Condition on the quotient even edge pattern. Every closed region cut permits
an independent internal cycle bit, so the forest probability is at most
  E product_i (1 - 1_{cut_i is zero}/2).
A cycle quotient gives exactly 1/2+2^(-k-1), so an exponential bound would fit
the standard extremal geometry. General linear-subspace models can have a
1/k error, but those models may not arise from graphical cut spaces.
A spanning tree with many marked leaves gives independent cut patterns after
choosing nonadjacent leaves. Long degree-two series chains instead carry one
common parity bit. A rigorous combination of these cases is being investigated.
This proposed bound is not yet proved and must not be cited as a theorem.
