Main statement checked
----------------------
If np - ln n - ln ln n tends to infinity, then with high probability
Pex(G(n,p)) = (1+o(1)) log_2 n. The displayed quantitative upper error
(4+o(1)) ln n / ln w, for w=min(np-ln n-ln ln n,ln ln n), also passes.

Independent checks
------------------
1. Exposure budget: four auxiliary probabilities D/n, D=w/100000, plus
   (ln n+ln ln n+w/2)/n sum to at most p eventually. The final random graph
   can therefore contain their independent union by monotone coupling.

2. Initial objects: all required lengths are O(ln n), the list has O(ln n)
   entries, and multiplicity is at most three. The cycle-list lemma gives
   vertex-disjoint objects. Their O((ln n)^2) vertices cannot cover all G1
   edges on the stated edge-count/maximum-degree event, so the reserved edge
   f exists. Including f in H1 before exposing G2 is essential and is done.

3. Chain freshness and rollback: expose G2 only inside V\H1. A candidate
   edge xy lies in a matching on an untouched cycle. Query x against the
   previous leaf set; condition on the resulting incoming path; temporarily
   exclude only this path before querying y against a resulting expander.
   The y-row is still fresh. A failed candidate does not remove the old
   leaf tree. After success, discard its unused branches before embedding
   the next tree. Thus there is no forbidden set containing two simultaneous
   linear-size trees, and the reservoir retains n-o(n) vertices. The failure
   sum over cycle lengths is geometric, so it is o(1) for every D->infinity.

4. Reusing the chosen endpoint of f costs at most H previously exposed
   incident edges. Another H fresh candidates exist in its n-o(n) reservoir.
   The other endpoint of f is used only by the final leaf-set query in G2.
   Both endpoint rows are fresh in the independent G4 used later.

5. Separate short-cycle chain: the chain through S3,...,ST attaches the
   binary block to the digit block without enlarging the binary base cycle.
   Its attachment at the designated endpoint of f is consistent with the
   later spanning path. This separation prevents circular parameter growth.

6. High binary detours: the compound two-tree structures have positive
   stems, maximum degree a+1, and fewer than n/50 vertices. The chosen
   candidate edges avoid low-bit endpoints, and have pairwise disjoint
   endpoints. The conditional failure bound needs no independence between
   trials: the expected number of failures and Markov's inequality suffice.
   Since ell_*/K tends to infinity, the candidate pool fits on C_*.

7. Digit detours: a cycle of length 8b+5 has at least 4b matching edges after
   avoiding the two endpoints of e_short. The stems 1+j and 1+jb are positive,
   preserving the tree degree bound. Choosing one successful detour from
   each group gives exactly T+1+j0+b*j1, hence [T+1,T+b^2]. This meets the
   binary interval because T=o(ell_*) and b^2>=2ell_*.

8. Spanning path: use all retained detours and long arcs of the S_j, while
   omitting f, e_short, and each selected edge of S_j from the spanning path.
   These omitted edges remain in the final subgraph. The binary block is a
   single contiguous segment of length L0+ell_*-1. All other constructed
   vertices form the adjoining short-cycle chain and digit segment. The two
   free endpoints are precisely the unused endpoints of f and e_short.
   The retained graph has at most 2L0 vertices; temporary trees do not count.

9. Hamilton completion at the exact threshold was checked directly against
   the revised primary source, not inferred from a stronger-density version:
   Yahav Alon and Michael Krivelevich, Sparse pancyclic subgraphs of random
   graphs, November 29, 2024, Lemma 2.1 (PDF page 3), and Claim 4.7 (page 11).
   https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~krivelev/pancyclic-rg.pdf
   Lemma 2.1 assumes Np-ln N-ln ln N->infinity and gives a set S of size N/4
   and, for each s in S, a set T_s of size N/4, with a Hamilton s-t path for
   every t in T_s. For X in this construction, the claimed lower bound
   (1/2-1/25-o(1))*w tends to infinity. Independent G4 endpoint rows attach to
   S and then T_s with failure o(1). No Hamilton-connectedness assertion or
   stronger threshold is needed.

10. Length coverage and excess: short cycles cover [3,T]; digits cover the
    next interval and overlap the binary interval [ell_*,ell_*+L0]; binary
    replacements on the Hamilton cycle cover [n-L0,n]. The original source's
    long-window construction applies because the whole binary segment is
    contiguous and ell_*=o(n^0.9)=o(L0). Its candidate counts and union bound
    remain valid with p5=D/n and m=o(ln n). All failed query edges are omitted
    from the chosen subgraph. Rank contributions give excess at most
    K+2b+T+m+O(1). Finally d~ln n/ln w, b=o(d), m=o(d), and T=4d+o(d), proving
    the quantitative error stated after the theorem.
