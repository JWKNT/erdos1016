The parallel-region lemma is correct. After including one link component
in the connected core, deleting any two other complementary components
leaves a connected remainder. Their zero-boundary events are independent.
Conditioning on both zero-boundary events gives independent uniform cycle
spaces inside the two regions. The corresponding nonempty internal-even
events are therefore pairwise independent. The second-moment bound gives
the stated count at most 2^(t+1), including the one omitted link component.

The revised link estimate has no dependence on the forest excess. Its
constant 2^(t+1) is large but fixed for each fixed exponent kappa.

All two-scale exponents check:
  Elementary case: delta=(1-3kappa)/8,
    d1/2-(d0+kappa)=delta and d1/2-kappa=3delta.
  Exponential-cut case: delta=(1-2kappa)/8,
    d1/2-d0=1/2-3delta>0 and d1/2-kappa=3delta.
The large deletion is o(r/log r), the small deletion is negligible against
the block count, and both exceptional-pair errors tend to zero.

The moment inequality with the two-link weight is correct:
  EZ^2 <= mu^2 + W2 + (1+eta)mu.
It gives W2>mu^2-(1+eta)mu and mu<(1+eta)/(4eps).
The far-pair subtraction and edge-adjacent-pair error leave a partner row
of weight at least eight when mu>=20. The maximal disjoint family has the
stated lower bound, and the nested-separator argument is unchanged.

Disconnected marked sets cause no gap. Choose one connected component as
the anchor. Every component of the entire marked set meets at most one
block, since all block boundary edges end on separator cycles outside P.
Discarding at most comp(Gamma[P]) blocks removes all marked vertices.

The restoration simplification is valid. A full block that misses S0
cannot have an edge to S0: every edge leaving it in Gamma-C ends on one of
its two separator cycles, and those cycles avoid S0. The same applies to P.
Discarding blocks meeting N(C) removes every edge to the deleted cycle C.
Thus the induced block has degree deficit at most 2L and girth>D0.

For the local supply step, choose, for example,

Perron term is at least 7/8 for all lengths at most L. Sum over even
lengths 6x<=ell<=L. Since v<=O(r), the nonreal spectral error is o(1).
The repeated-walk error is O(L^2 2^(-s0)) times the block cycle mass and
tends to zero uniformly. The resulting cycle mass has a fixed positive

No eigenvalue multiplicity statement or block nonadjacency is needed.
