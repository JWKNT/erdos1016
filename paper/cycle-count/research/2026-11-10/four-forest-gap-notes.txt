Four-terminal forest saturation: independent counterexample search.
10 November 2026.

Status: the existence of a positive uniform saturation gap remains open.
This search proves only an upper bound on any such gap.

A simple normalized four-pole of rank eight has 255 forest states among
its 256 affine terminal joins. Exactly one state contains a cycle.
The complete graph is in four-forest-gap-switch-rank8.jsonl.
The independent Python checker enumerates all 256 states and checks parity.
Its output is four-forest-gap-independent-checks.json.
Thus any uniform gap must be at most 1/256, improving the previous 1/128.

The bridge-and-diamond operation preserves this density at arbitrarily
large ranks. Each operation adds a full rank-two terminal-path component.
Every affine join uses the bridge and restricts to one of four paths there.
Both forest and total state counts therefore multiply by four.
The independent checker verifies the first three extensions.
This operation does not give densities tending to one.

The witness came from a degree-preserving two-edge-switch walk.
The rank-eight walk used 250000 proposals and accepted 56094 states.
It found the unique-bad-state witness at proposal 94408.
Each printed witness receives a complete exact affine-space count.
The search is not exhaustive and gives no maximum-density certificate.
Rank-ten and rank-twelve walks also found density255/256.

All 300 extensions of this rank-eight witness were checked.
An extension subdivides two different edges and joins the new vertices.
None had exactly one bad state. This excludes only those 300 extensions.
It does not exclude other rank-nine graphs or different constructions.
Further walks used 2 million rank-nine proposals and 1 million rank-eleven
proposals. Neither improved the deficit 1/256. Those runs are stopped.
The rank-eight witness has girth four. The larger scaled witnesses have
girth three. They do not test a possible gap under large-girth assumptions.

A separate finite census deletes every pair of nonincident edges from two
explicit cubic graphs. Their girths are checked directly by breadth-first
search. The girth-six graph has14 vertices and gives168 four-poles of rank6.
Their largest forest density is61/64. The girth-seven graph has24 vertices
and gives558 four-poles of rank11. Their largest density is457/512.
These classes are not exhaustive among graphs of their rank or girth.
See four-forest-gap-high-girth.cpp/.jsonl for the graphs and exact counts.

Potential structural direction, not a theorem:
Join two saturated two-poles through a bridge between subdivided interior
edges. With the four original terminals, every terminal join is a forest.
Its bridge is absent, since each shore contains two terminals.
If the attachments lie deep inside long saturated ladders, none of the
six terminal-path coordinates must have positive limiting density.
Thus a full forest sector need not give a full terminal-path coordinate.
Adding another connection could create rare bad states. This needs an exact
state calculation; no asymptotic near-full construction is claimed.
