The bounded-overlap interval sum is valid in all three cases.
For disjoint intervals, the two lengths share one free split position.
Outer gaps and the middle gap have summable geometric weights.
Crossing intervals have bounded overlap length and one free meeting position.
For nested intervals, the inner interval has bounded length and one free position.
These give O_B((D+1)*2^(D/2)) for the ordered interval sum.

Two disjoint cycles cannot both cross an ordinary two-vertex layer.
Thus every common interior layer lies in the bounded initial marked set.
At most four additional layers are interval endpoints.
Marking endpoints adds at most eight vertices, uniformly in the rank.
The previous nonconsecutive matching encoding then applies unchanged.
Its geometric weight is bounded by2^((|I|+|J|)/2).

The series-piece estimates are valid.
The weighted one-piece sum is at most(b+2)*2^(b/2).
For pairs in different pieces, the normalized sum is at most
binom(q,2)*2^(-(q-2)/2), whose maximum is15/4<4.
The capping injection therefore gives the asserted linear forest bound.

The universal four-pole aggregate only used the fourth-degree factor in
the cross-sum forest estimate. Replacing it gives a linear aggregate error.
All fixed low-profile and bounded-rank cases retain their earlier margins.

For aligned full K2,2 shores, the same-side cap pairing agrees across the cut.
The exact gluing formula subtracts its product C_h*C_k.
The remaining forest contribution is bounded by
C_h*D_k+D_h*C_k+D_h*D_k.
The audited saturated bounds make this O((h+k)*2^((h+k)/2)).
The other path coordinates and internal cycles have half-exponent bounds.
Aligned stars already had a constant half-exponent error.
All nonaligned and ordinary profiles retain fixed deficits.

Bounded small shores now use a linear aggregate error instead of degree four.
Their bounded rank factor is absorbed into the absolute constant.
The exact C4 low-profile case retains its positive absorption margin.

The cyclic-square lower count is correct, including intervals that use all
blocks but omit one interface. Its all-used sector has no double-cap block,
because two disjoint same-side caps do not fit inside one square.
The total is
  2^(2q)+(2q+1/2)*2^q-3q,
with rank2q+1 and a cyclic four-edge cut around each square.
Thus the linear rank factor is necessary within the theorem's class.
