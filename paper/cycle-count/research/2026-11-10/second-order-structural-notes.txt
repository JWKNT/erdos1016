NEW COMPLETE ARGUMENT, INDEPENDENT AUDITS PASSED:
Exponential small-cut decay is proved in second-order-structural-notes.tex.
For k disjoint connected cyclic regions with cut size at most fixed d>=2,
their simultaneous forest probability is at most
  1/2 + exp(-k / [2^d (2^((d-1)(2^d-1))-1)]).
For d<=1 the stronger bound 2^-k holds.

The two ingredients are a direct-sum/common-functional dichotomy and a
graphic series-class argument. Local star-coordinate spaces have dimension
at most d-1. A direct sum of 2^d such spaces gives probability below 1/2.
Otherwise a nonzero functional belongs to a linear number of local spaces.
Split each selected star to represent that functional by one new edge.
All new edge coordinates coincide. Removing them leaves exactly as many
components as new edges, with a cycle as quotient. Conditioned on the common
coordinate being zero, the component cycle spaces are independent.
A matching in the quotient cycle gives independent closed-star events.

The quotient reduction from the original cyclic regions is exact. Its
kernel is the direct sum of their internal cycle spaces. Conditional on a
closed boundary, a cyclic region is a forest with probability at most 1/2.
This supplies the product of closed-star penalties used by the theorem.
Root and path_stability independently audited all steps and constants.
