\begin{abstract}
Every connected loopless cubic multigraph of cycle rank $r$ with a cyclic edge cut of size at most six satisfies
\[
 c(G)\le2^{r-1}+K r2^{r/2}
\]
for an absolute constant $K$.
The known Horak--Kim family shows that the error has sharp order within this class.
For large rank, a cyclic six-cut without a smaller cyclic cut gives the stronger strict inequality $c(G)<2^{r-1}$.
Two large shores are handled by incompatible terminal-pairing supports.
For a bounded shore, a $K_{3,3}$ subdivision forces a missing four-terminal forest state.
Its integral counting deficit absorbs the internal cycles of the other shore.
The unrestricted sharp bound remains open.
\end{abstract}
\section*{Status and scope}
All new arguments in this report passed independent mathematical audits by the root and three research agents.
Exact computations separately check the state identities, the six-cycle certificate, and the subdivision witnesses.
The new theorems are not formalized in Lean.
Publication priority has not been established.
The earlier five-cut proof and the unrestricted forest machinery are explicit inputs, stated below.
This report records the new extension; it does not reproduce those earlier proofs.

Rank means $|E|-|V|+\operatorname{comp}$.
Paths and cycles are simple, with parallel edges distinguished.
A cyclic cut has a cycle on each shore.
A normalized $k$-pole has $k$ distinct degree-two ports and all other degrees three.
An earlier relaxation obstruction is included because the new cap inequalities explain its failure.

\begin{theorem}[Established five-cut bound]\label{thm:sharp-order-five-cut-error}
There is an absolute $K_5$ such that every connected loopless cubic multigraph of rank $r$ with a cyclic cut of size at most five satisfies $c(G)\le2^{r-1}+K_5r2^{r/2}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Established forest and cycle estimates]
For connected subcubic graphs of unbounded rank, cubic outside a fixed connected marked set, the probability that a uniform even edge set is a forest outside that set is at most $1/2+o(1)$.
Also, uniformly over graphs of rank $h$, one has $c(G)\le(1/2+o(1))2^h$.
The separate unrestricted packet proves a stronger quantitative estimate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{lemma}[Established saturated two-pole classification]\label{lem:full-two-pole-classification}
A saturated normalized two-pole consists of bridgeless saturated pieces joined by bridges in series.
Each piece is a digon or a capped permutation ladder.
Its permutation is a concatenation of singleton blocks and adjacent transpositions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{theorem}[Established path saturation gap]\label{thm:terminal-path-saturation-gap}
For a connected graph of rank $h$ and distinct terminals $s,t$, either $P_{st}=2^h$ or $P_{st}\le(15/16)2^h$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Established protected high-density theorem]\label{thm:high-density-protected-cyclic-sets}
Fix the number of ports, a bound on the number of protected vertices, and $\varepsilon>0$.
At sufficiently large rank, terminal-path density at least $1/2+\varepsilon$ in a normalized pole gives two disjoint cyclic vertex sets.
They avoid every port and protected vertex, and have boundary at most four in every cubic completion.
The rank threshold depends only on the fixed parameters.
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Aggregate forest states]\label{thm:fixed-port-aggregate}
For a normalized $k$-pole $Y$ of rank $h$, with fixed $k$, let $F_S$ count forests with odd-degree set $S$.
Then, uniformly as $h\to\infty$,
\[
 2^{-h}\sum_{S\text{ even}}F_S\le2^{k-2}+o(1).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Attach a fixed trivalent tree with $k$ leaves to the $k$ ports, using one edge per leaf.
Mark the entire added tree.
The graph has rank $h+k-1$, and all vertices outside the marked set are cubic.
Every even port pattern has a unique extension through the tree, determined by parity across its edge cuts.
Each port parity class has $2^h$ members.
The forest probability outside the marked set is therefore $2^{-(h+k-1)}\sum_SF_S$.
Apply the established marked forest estimate and multiply by $2^{k-1}$.
\end{proof}

\section{Verification and remaining work}
The exact six-cut checker verifies 960 complete gluings and 180 two-edge augmentation identities.
Two independent solver-free checks verify the six-cycle coefficient certificate.
The new subdivision checker tests all 3,003 assignments of six ports to the nine $K_{3,3}$ core paths.
It constructs a nonforest four-port parity witness for each assignment and checks 48,048 affine parity states.
It also checks 500 outside-attachment fixtures, including components with several attachments.
These checks support the finite identities; the asymptotic conclusions follow from the proofs above.

The unrestricted $O(r2^{r/2})$ bound is still open.
The established reductions now allow the remaining large cubic kernels to be assumed cyclically seven-edge-connected, with girth at least seven.
The strongest unrestricted estimate remains
\[
 \psi(r)\le2^{r-1}+O\!\left(\frac{2^r(\log_2 r)^{11/2}}{\sqrt r}\right).
\]
The shared live handoff records subsequent partial work separately.
