Local six-sector and critical seven-cut checkpoint.
10 November 2026.

The all-fixed-cut theorem now follows from the independent deletion induction
in path-fixed-cut-induction.tex. Its written proof passed our full audit.
The local optimization below is no longer needed for that theorem.
It remains a separate finite combinatorial lead.

In the local model, each shore has six-terminal forest mass one.
Corresponding four-terminal totals are common Boolean variables.
Corresponding four-pairing supports are disjoint across the two shores.
Positive six-pairings across shores have no common pair.
All path coordinates vanish.

Two canonical compatible six-matchings have mass at least1/15, one per shore.
This is a valid symmetry reduction: choose a largest coordinate on each shore.
Their positive pairings are compatible, and every compatible ordered pair is
equivalent under a permutation of the six ports.

Numerical optimization of the number of active four-sectors gave:
  matching augmentation only:11;
  matching plus star caps:9;
  matching plus binary-tree caps:10;
  matching, star and binary-tree caps:9.
All four runs reported optimality within five seconds.
These upper bounds are not certified by an exact dual or exhaustive proof.
No theorem in this work relies on their numerical optimality.

Every saved feasible witness passed an independent rational primal check.
The checker uses standard-library Fraction arithmetic and reconstructs the
matching, star, binary, compatibility and sector-total inequalities.
Run python3 four-six-verify-local-witnesses.py.
The output is four-six-local-witness-checks.json.
It verifies seven witnesses, including the relaxations below.

Relaxing four-pairing support disjointness to coordinatewise x+y<=1 gives
maximum11 with star caps, and12 with matching augmentation alone.
The first witness has one pure six-pairing and two pairings sharing an edge
on the other shore. Their weights are1/2 each.
Thus the relaxation fails even with a two-coordinate support reduction.

A support-only Boolean model retains nonempty disjoint four-pairing supports,
the required positive slots from matching augmentation, and star triangles.
It has an exactly checked feasible assignment with12 active four-sectors.
