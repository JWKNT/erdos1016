Status: PASS. No mathematical gap found in the current full proof.

1. Parameters and uniformity.
All constants A,t,kappa are fixed, positive, and independent of r.
Their enormous size only increases the eventual threshold.
The short-cycle cut bound is O(x^2 r^(1/100)) or O(x^2 r^(1/10)).
Multiplication by the cycle length gives the stated deletion sizes.
The marked-set bound makes L*d_j/v_j=O(A/x)+o(1), which tends to zero.
The smaller-scale exceptional-pair error is bounded by a constant times
x^3*r^(1/1000-1/200), so it tends to zero uniformly.
The fixed tree-link threshold eventually satisfies 2t+D_j/2<=D_j.

2. Nested cycle deletions.
The first maximal disjoint family can be extended to the second.
Each residual graph is simple and has the claimed girth.
For either family, a tree component in the link proof cannot meet a deleted
cycle. Each deleted cycle is connected, avoids the two tested cycles, and
would otherwise lie entirely in that tree component.
Thus the original high-girth tree-link argument remains valid at both scales.

3. Moments.
The normalized cycle variables have the stated means and second moments.
The exceptional-partner lemma gives a uniform o(mu_j) remainder.
Subtracting the ordered one-link weight is valid.
Cauchy--Schwarz gives both
  Lambda_j < (1+eta_r)mu_j
and
  mu_j <= (1+eta_r)/(4 epsilon).
The stronger factor1-2epsilon can safely be discarded in the latter bound.
The spectral supply is at least21-o(1), so the stated lower bound20 holds.

4. Weighted extraction and nesting.
The far relation is antisymmetric: a path from P to its first vertex of C
avoids every far component behind C. The connected cycle C then lies in the
P-component after deleting a disjoint far cycle C'.
Intersecting and adjacent partners have total ordered weight o(mu_1).
The remaining weighted average is at least mu_1/2-1-o(1), hence exceeds8.
The pigeonhole over at mostL neighbors is valid.
A maximal disjoint separator family covers every separator cycle, so the
local mass estimate gives the stated polynomial lower bound on its size.
The connected-separator argument gives a linear order and nested U_i.

5. Full blocks and the two-scale restoration.
Each full block U_b minus(U_a union C_a) has boundary only on C_a,C_b in H.
It contains all intermediate separator cycles and avoids P and its neighbors.
Blocks separated by an unused separator cycle are nonadjacent.
At mostL blocks meet N(C). At most4|S0| blocks meet S0 or its neighbors.
This loss is o(r^(1/20)/x^4).
All surviving blocks lie in J0 and have zero degree deficit there.
Vertices of S1 minus S0 inside a block are restored, so their deleted
incidences do not create the old deficit obstruction.

6. Spectral test.
The displayed quadratic-form identity is exact.
The condition delta+3boundary<=theta*volume makes it nonpositive for
theta<=1/2. Nonadjacency eliminates every off-diagonal test-space term.
M(2) is positive semidefinite, and M'(lambda) is positive definite above3/2.
Thus the count of real roots includes multiplicity correctly.
The kernel-to-nonbacktracking map is injective for these roots.
The nonreal eigenvalue contribution has the stated uniform lower bound.
The block volume leaves a large margin in the theta condition.
The even window6x..24x gives a fixed multiple greater thanm/16 before the
vanishing repeated-walk correction. The nonreal error is o(1).
The polynomial lower bound onmu0 contradicts its O(r^kappa) upper bound.

7. Transfer to arbitrary cycle counts.
Suppressing degree-two vertices and deleting irrelevant trees preserves
rank and cycle count. A positive-rank core of minimum degree three has at
most2r-2 vertices and a cycle of lengthO(log r).
Uniform subdivision into three edges makes loops and parallel edges simple.
It preserves rank, cycle count, and the O(log r) witness length.
The marked cubic reduction has connected nonemptyP with sizeO(log r).
Flipping the witness cycle pairs forest-outside configurations with opposite
values on any chosen witness edge. Hence the through-edge cycle count is
at most half of the forest-configuration count.
The chosen edge is nonbridging, so deletion preserves connectedness and
decreases rank by one. The edge recurrence and geometric convolution give
the stated leading error coefficient1+o(1).

Editorial suggestion only: specify subdivision into three edges explicitly.
One subdivision does not remove a loop's parallel-edge representation.
The current phrase 'if necessary, subdivide uniformly to work with a simple
graph' permits the valid three-edge operation and is not a proof gap.
