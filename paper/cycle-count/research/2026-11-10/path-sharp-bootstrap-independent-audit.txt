The focused local proof needs only the original fixed-error forest input:
for sufficiently large rank rho, one marked cycle W with
|E(W)|<=rho/log^2(rho) has outside-EDGE forest probability at most11/20.
This is the original manuscript thm:forest together with rem:range, at a fixed
sufficiently large threshold. It does not require the later quantitative
endpoint forest bound or any sharp-order cycle estimate.

The edge/vertex distinction is handled explicitly. In the localized cap,
deleting the marked cycle EDGES leaves only pendant spokes outside the
retained interior. Those spokes cannot create a cycle. Thus its outside-edge
forest event is exactly the interior forest event needed by localization.
To use the simple-graph input, subdivide only non-W loops and parallel edges;
rank, the marked cycle size, and the outside-forest law are preserved.
