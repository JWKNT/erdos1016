Checkpoint: quantitative path rank-distance investigation.

The unrestricted conjectural scale is
positive normalized excess <= poly(b)*2^(-(b-dist)/2).
The Horak--Kim construction in second-order-constructions.tex proves that
an absolute constant prefactor is impossible. Its linear obstruction is
already audited; do not claim it as a new result here.

New exact transfer calculation is in
four-high-connectivity-path-transfers.tex.
Repeated triangle expansion at the vertex incident with a fixed edge f
has count P_j=2^j Q+A+(j+1)Z. The affine-coordinate bound Q<=2^(b-1)
means this family either eventually falls below half or has only
O(j*2^-j) normalized excess. It cannot refute the conjectured half
exponent at bounded terminal distance.

The critical case is equivalent to a connected four-pole K=N-f whose
entire four-terminal affine class is forest, with one pairing absent.
Adding f then gives one fully saturated half of the terminal-join class.
The remaining path count in K is the correction term. A structural
classification of these saturated slices could be useful, but no such
classification is proved here.

Finite diagnostic, not a general theorem:
I independently decoded the maximum-scaled-excess witnesses in the
path agent's complete cubic catalogue through order20. Their best
two-poles at ranks2,3,4 have a disjoint edge f with a saturated
f=1 path slice. The selected maxima at ranks5 through10 have none.
Thus the exact saturated-slice class alone does not explain all current
small-order extremal witnesses. No larger enumeration is warranted yet.

An exact affine decomposition supplied by path_stability is
sum over terminal paths P of 2^beta(N-V(P)) = 2^b.
It implies positive excess is at most half the number of paths with
forest complement. Counting those paths alone may lose the needed
cancellation: Hamilton paths are included, and cubic Hamilton-path
families can already exceed the half-exponent count. This is a warning
about that approach, not a counterexample to the signed excess bound.
