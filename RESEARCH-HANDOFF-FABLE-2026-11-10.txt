Items 1 and 2 of the outline. Short form:

1. Theorem 1.1 (cycle count) follows from the Entringer-Slater identity
   c(G) = c(G-e) + c_e(G) and your edge bound c_e(G) <= p(G,W) 2^(r-1), with e on a
   shortest cycle W of the kernel. This gives psi(r) <= psi(r-1) + (1/4+o(1)) 2^r.
   No chords, one component, same error term. See notes, section 1.

2. Hence X_r = psi(r) - 2^(r-1) satisfies X_r - X_(r-1) <= g(r), where g(r) is the
   largest value over graphs of the smallest excess c_e(G) - 2^(r-2) over edges.
   The 2^(-k) barrier of the chord proof is absent. See section 2.

3. Localisation lemma: for a connected vertex set R that avoids V(W),
   p(G,W) <= p(G_R, W_R), where W_R is ONE cycle with |delta R| edges and
   rank(G_R) = beta(G[R]) + |delta R|. See section 3.

4. General linear path stability (Problem 8.2), conditional on the forest estimate
   for one cycle with at most r/log^2 r edges (Remark 6.10) or on your Theorem
   research-marked-size. The proof uses your splitting lemma without planarity,
   distance layers from u, a greedy window, item 3, and the small-cut lemma.
   See section 4. PLEASE AUDIT: Lemma 3.1 and Lemma 4.4 first.

5. The goal "one marked cycle with |E(W)| <= c r gives p <= 1/2 + eps" is false.
   There are cubic graphs with a cycle W of 3(r-1)/4 edges and p(G,W) -> 1.
   Tutte-Coxeter example: r = 61, |E(W)| = 45, p = 0.9985. Heuristically the
   estimate fails already at C r / log r, so your range is sharp up to the constant.
   See section 5.

6. Conjecture A: P_uv(N) <= 2^(b-1) + C 2^((b+l)/2). It would give
   psi(r) = 2^(r-1) + Theta(r 2^(r/2)), which matches Horak-Kim. A random search
   at ranks up to 7 puts a fixed C in doubt: at distance two the best excess over
   2^(b-1) is 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 18 for b = 2..7. An exhaustive search would decide.
   See section 6.

IDEA for your overlapping-region branch, in case it is useful (not proved, not checked):
In the terminal-forest setting, do not pick one class. The classes are the suppressed edges of the
terminal forest, and their functionals a_e are pairwise distinct and nonzero, so the bits a_e are
pairwise independent. If mu_e is the weight of class e and f = sum of mu_e over the classes with
a_e = 0, then E exp(-c f) <= 1/2 + exp(-c' sum mu_e): if one class has at least 1/16 of the total
weight, condition on its bit; otherwise Chebyshev gives P(f < total/4) <= 1/4. This would replace
the factor 1/(2B) for the number of classes by a constant, if the regions on different suppressed
edges are conditionally independent given all terminal coordinates. I have not checked that last
condition.

    cycle, of length g >= 5. Then G has two disjoint cycles that each contain an edge of W.
