UNRESTRICTED SHARP-ORDER BOUND: MATHEMATICAL PROOF COMPLETED
For an absolute constant K and every finite graph of rank r>=1,
  c(G) <= 2^(r-1) + K*r*2^(r/2).
The known Horak-Kim cyclic-square family has excess Theta(r*2^(r/2)),
so the order is necessary. The optimal constant and exact psi(r) remain open.

This is a written mathematical working proof with independent internal
line audits. It is not yet a complete Lean formalization or an externally

THE DECISIVE NEW ARGUMENT
1. Localization works for a disconnected retained region whenever its
   complement is connected. A cap cycle preserves the full interior law.
   Removing cap-cycle EDGES leaves pendant spokes, so the original marked-
   cycle forest theorem applies to the interior forest event exactly.
2. A connected BFS-tree marker for all ports has at most k vertices per layer.
   Localization and the growing-window argument bound each layer by B(k+1)^2
   whenever an affine forest sector has density at least .6. Only the original
   qualitative forest theorem at fixed error .05 is needed.
3. A weighted-kernel Moore bound supplies a large port-free ball. It gives
   conditional affine forest density <=.6 for either of two core regimes:
   (a) rank>=256, ports<=4D, and girth>=D above a fixed absolute threshold;
   (b) rank>=L, ports<=2L, and girth>=C0*log2(L+2).
4. The quotient star-space argument gives long classes of articulation
   vertices with identical actual terminal bipartitions. Split a long class
   into disjoint middle regions of rank>=256 and boundary<=4D. At most|S|+1
   regions meet the previous deletion S or the connected marker P. Two clean
   regions would give forest probability <=(.6)^2=.36, a contradiction.
   Therefore the next cycle packing is bounded independently of its excess.
5. Iterating maximal packings on common dyadic scales gives
     log2(|S(D)|+1) <= 2D + O(log2(D+2)^2).
   At D0 in[sqrt(log2 r),2sqrt(log2 r)), S0=r^o(1).
   At D1 in[(log2 r)/16,(log2 r)/8), S1<=r^(1/4+o(1)).
6. Fable's parallel-link and nested-chain machinery applies at D1. Its first
   moment conclusion needs only forest probability>1/2. It produces at least
   a constant times r^(1/32)/(log r)^3 nested separators. Restoring medium
   deletions yields many blocks of order>=8L^2 and boundary<=2L, L=A*log2 r.
   After discarding r^o(1) dirty blocks, two clean blocks remain. Their
   independent conditional affine probabilities again give .36<.5.
7. Thus the marked forest probability is EXACTLY <=1/2 above a fixed girth
   threshold, for connected markers of size<=3log2 r at sufficiently large rank.
   Each edge of a shortest cycle then lies on at most 2^(r-2) cycles.
   General-graph rank induction, the sharp <=4-cut theorem, and the bounded-
   girth deficit prove the unrestricted result.
